# Ranger video, version 2

3:15, 1920x1080, 30 fps, with a male voice-over (American English). The video file is 48.6 MB (under 50 MB).
Built in CutWire Drift 0.8.0 (headless) with the `cutwire-drift` skill.

## Notes

- **Story scenes are illustrations**, drawn in code (`build_tools/anim2.py`), because stock video sites are blocked in the build environment. To swap in real footage later, replace the `sc_*.mp4` clips on the full-frame video lane in Drift.
- **Component images are drawn stand-ins.** The real photos in `components.json` (LCSC, EBYTE, flyingtech) could not be downloaded in the build environment, so `assets/components/` is empty. Download them yourself and replace the `part_*.png` cards if you want the real photos.
- **Voice-over** is the open-source Kokoro text-to-speech model, voice `am_michael` (Apache 2.0 licence), generated by `build_tools/vo.py`.
- **Figures**: 8 km is the module maker's open line-of-sight rating (shown on screen as "Open line of sight"). 202 kbps is the top LoRa data rate of the SX1280 chip inside the E28 module, reached at short range (long range uses slower rates). The mesh "reach" counter is hops × up to 8 km. Rs. 3,000 is the estimated mass-production cost you gave.
- Music and sound effects are synthesised (`build_tools/audio2.py`), so no licence is needed.
