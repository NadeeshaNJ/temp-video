# Ranger video, version 2

3:10, 1920x1080, 30 fps, with a male voice-over (American English).
Built in CutWire Drift 0.8.0 (headless) with the `cutwire-drift` skill.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `exports/ranger_v2_1080p.mp4` | Finished video, H.264 + AAC, mastered to -14 LUFS |
| `exports/ranger_v2.srt` | Subtitles of the voice-over, with times |
| `drift_project/ranger_v2.drift` | Editable Drift project with all media packed inside. Open it in Drift. |
| `drift_project/new_assets/` | The media new in v2 as loose files: illustrated scenes, part cards, voice-over lines, music |
| `components.json` | The parts list (name, role, caption, specs, image link, source) |
| `build_tools/` | Scripts that made the v2 scenes, part cards, voice-over, music and timeline |

The v1 assets (OLED screens, phone screens, footage cards, hero cut-outs) are in `../drift_project/assets/`.

## Notes

- **Story scenes are illustrations**, drawn in code (`build_tools/anim2.py`), because stock video sites are blocked in the build environment. To swap in real footage later, replace the `sc_*.mp4` clips on the full-frame video lane in Drift.
- **Component images are drawn stand-ins**, labelled "Parts shown as illustrations" in the video. The real photos in `components.json` (LCSC, EBYTE, flyingtech) could not be downloaded in the build environment, so `assets/components/` is empty. Download them yourself and replace the `part_*.png` cards if you want the real photos.
- **Voice-over** is the open-source Kokoro text-to-speech model, voice `am_michael` (Apache 2.0 licence), generated by `build_tools/vo.py`.
- **Figures**: 8 km is the module maker's open line-of-sight rating. 202 kbps is the top LoRa data rate of the SX1280 chip inside the E28 module, reached at short range (long range uses slower rates). The mesh "reach" counter is an illustration (hops × up to 8 km). Rs. 3,000 is the estimated mass-production cost you gave.
- Music and sound effects are synthesised (`build_tools/audio2.py`), so no licence is needed.
